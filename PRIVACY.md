# Privacy and patient data

The app collects the contact details patients provide, their preferred time, selected service where applicable, request status, and consent metadata. Staff use this information to manage appointments. The website language cookie remembers a single locale. Tab-scoped browser storage can retain an unfinished appointment form across refreshes and language switches and is cleared on successful submission. Patients using a shared browser should close the appointment tab after use.

When enabled, analytics records aggregate event counts by date and language. Review the deployment settings and privacy text before enabling it.

Limit access to named clinic staff and minimize what is recorded. Ask for separate consent before sending marketing messages. A missed booking or clinic communication is handled by staff. Use [OWNER_SETUP_CHECKLIST.md](OWNER_SETUP_CHECKLIST.md) to approve the policy text, contact routes, retention schedule, and any third-party provider before deployment.

Keep appointment details and status references out of logs, analytics, and support tickets. Apply the clinic's retention policy to the active database, staff exports, and backups. If a patient requests access or correction, verify their identity through an approved channel before sharing data. The clinic should review its obligations under applicable Indian privacy law with qualified counsel.
