# Clinic owner setup checklist

- Verify the clinic's public name, phone, address, map pin, and holiday calendar.
- Confirm working hours, appointment duration, booking horizon, and how long pending requests hold a slot. Add breaks as gaps between working periods and add closure dates.
- Verify the doctor's name and credentials against the applicable professional records before publishing them. Confirm fees and other clinic details with the owner.
- Approve English and Kannada medical treatment and FAQ drafts with the doctor before release. Have a fluent Kannada reviewer check navigation and patient instructions.
- Confirm rights to any actual clinic photos. Clearly distinguish any illustrative imagery from real clinic photos.
- Enroll named staff and verify MFA, least-privilege roles and access recovery. Practice handling a new appointment through closure.
- Approve privacy and retention policies, public contact for patient requests, and any measurement or messaging providers.
- Control the domain, hosting, backup keys and billing using clinic-owned accounts with documented recovery contacts.
- Confirm HTTPS, alerting, backup retention, and isolated restoration before accepting patient data.
- Review the [SEO migration plan](SEO_MIGRATION.md) and verify both locales and all redirects before DNS cutover.

## Redesign release review

In the owner gallery, upload rights-cleared clinic images and provide descriptive text in both languages. In the clinic profile, select the homepage image and the doctor's portrait, then verify the biography, registration and facilities before publishing. The default homepage uses an illustrative dental model. The clinical team must approve each treatment translation, including its risks and aftercare, and configure working periods before opening bookings to patients. Sample services and availability on port 3001 are confined to the local demo.
